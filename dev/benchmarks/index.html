Measured on an AVX2 laptop (Intel Core Ultra 7 268V, 4 performance and 4 efficiency cores) unless noted; fresh input every round, methods interleaved in random order, best of the rounds. The tables with all cases are in the README; the scripts are in bench/ (bench.jl, types.jl, crossover.jl, tables.jl, plots.jl).
